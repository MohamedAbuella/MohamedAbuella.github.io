--- title: "PyPSA-Libya: National Decarbonization and Hydrogen Export Pathways" created_at: 2026-10-28 00:00:00 EST author: Mohamed Abuella layout: post tags: [Blog] ---

This project introduces PyPSA-Libya, a high-resolution, open-source energy system model designed to explore optimal transition pathways for the Libyan power sector and evaluate large-scale cross-border hydrogen export to Europe. Built as a country-specific extension of the global PyPSA-Earth framework, the model provides a transparent and reproducible benchmark for national decarbonization.

The study investigates two primary frameworks: an electricity-only capacity expansion analysis comparing a zero-emission constraint against an unconstrained baseline at a 36-cluster resolution, and a sector-coupled hydrogen export study assessing the levelized cost of exporting 70 TWh/year of hydrogen under varying technological and policy scenarios, including a 100 EUR/tonne CO2 carbon tax regime.

Key Findings & Insights

  • Renewable Potential: High-irradiance southern inland nodes and coastal corridors heavily favor solar PV and onshore wind buildouts.
  • Storage & Flexibility: Full domestic decarbonization significantly increases short-duration battery storage requirements to manage diurnal solar ramps.
  • Blue vs. Green Hydrogen: Under carbon pricing regimes, steam methane reforming with carbon capture (SMR-CC) frequently out-competes pure green electrolysis, offering a lower-cost compliance pathway for cross-border export utilizing Libya's domestic hydrocarbon infrastructure.
  • Water Sourcing: Seawater reverse osmosis (SWRO) desalination co-located at coastal clusters satisfies water requirements with a negligible impact on overall system costs and energy demand.
